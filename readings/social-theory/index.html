Social theory

General sociological theory: action, structure, rational choice, critical theory and their successors.

Of its 92 readings, 2 are assigned in two or more sources and listed first, with the number of sources beside each (in bold for the core readings, in 4 or more); the 90 listed in only one source are folded under each heading.
