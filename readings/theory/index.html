Theory and theorizing

What counts as theory, how theoretical contributions are made, and the state of the field.

Of its 58 readings, 21 are assigned in two or more sources and listed first, with the number of sources beside each (in bold for the core readings, in 4 or more); the 37 listed in only one source are folded under each heading.

Drawn from: Fiss (USC); Clough (UBC); Maoret (IESE); Glaser (Alberta); Pollock (Tennessee); Wan (CUHK); Sullivan (HKUST); Lu, Li, Park, Yang & Yan (CityU); Amit (Wharton); Yiu (CUHK); Jia (USC); Cameron (Wharton); Marquis (HBS); Peng (UT Dallas); Anteby (Boston University); Zhou (HKU).
