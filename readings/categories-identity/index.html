Categories, identity and authenticity

Market categories, organizational identity and authenticity.

Of its 97 readings, 30 are assigned in two or more sources and listed first, with the number of sources beside each (in bold for the core readings, in 4 or more); the 67 listed in only one source are folded under each heading.

In the theory tree: grows out of 6 Organizational ecology and demography, 9 Institutional theory and 13 Status and social evaluation.
