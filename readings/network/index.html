How the readings are assigned together

A map of the 575 readings in two or more sources. Two readings are linked when a syllabus assigns them in the same session; the figure draws the 905 links that recur in at least two syllabi (5,123 in all place the points). Readings that are taught together sit close together, so the clusters are the streams as the 30 syllabi themselves draw them, not as I drew them.

Colour is the stream's family in the theory tree; size is the number of sources. Click or tap a reading to see it and what it is most often assigned with; the lists below the figure give the same in words.
