Organizational ecology and demography

Founding, mortality, inertia, niches and density dependence.

Of its 80 readings, 34 are assigned in two or more sources and listed first, with the number of sources beside each (in bold for the core readings, in 4 or more); the 46 listed in only one source are folded under each heading.

In the theory tree: grows out of 4 Contingency and configurational theory; leads to 7 Entrepreneurship and new organizations and 10 Categories, identity and authenticity.
