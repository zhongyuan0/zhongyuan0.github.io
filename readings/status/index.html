Status and social evaluation

Status, reputation, and how audiences evaluate actors in markets.

Of its 96 readings, 18 are assigned in two or more sources and listed first, with the number of sources beside each (in bold for the core readings, in 4 or more); the 78 listed in only one source are folded under each heading.
