孙中源

Zhongyuan Sun

Specially Appointed Assistant Professor Hitotsubashi Institute for Advanced Study Hitotsubashi University, Tokyo

I study strategy, innovation and entrepreneurship, drawing on sociology and philosophy. My research is organized around three classic ideals: the true, the good, and the beautiful. In management, these correspond to authenticity, social issues in management, and aesthetics.

I received my PhD in Management from the City University of Hong Kong. Methodologically, I began with quantitative research and have since expanded into theoretical and qualitative work, at both the individual and firm levels.

If our interests align, I welcome the opportunity to collaborate. Please feel free to reach out to me by email.
